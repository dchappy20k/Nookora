## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the hushed, tactile atmosphere of a private architectural salon after twilight. It balances severe spatial discipline with the warmth of aged materials, patinated bronze, and diffuse low-level lighting. Built for architects, curated collectors, interior visionaries, and patron-level clientele, the aesthetic is deliberately non-transactional: it behaves like an archival monographic journal rather than a utility interface.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with an **Editorial Architectural Aesthetic**. Large, measured fields of deep chromatic charcoal and blackened pine create an intentional negative space that allows high-resolution spatial photography and sculpted typography to breathe. Depth is conveyed strictly through tonal stacking, diffused ambient warmth, and structural hairline seams—eschewing synthetic blurs, plastic glows, or heavy decorative drop shadows. Every touchpoint signals permanence, restraint, and calculated composure.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in low-reflectance vegetal blacks and burnished metallic tones, evoking an architectural space illuminated only by indirect museum sconces.

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#111714`)**: Midnight Black. The deepest canvas floor, infused with an imperceptible dark olive-forest undertone to avoid synthetic, sterile black.
- **Section Ground (`#18211C`)**: Deep Forest. Structural background zones and full-bleed framing containers.
- **Elevated Surface (`#202A24`)**: Elevated modules, top bars, persistent rails, and subtle structural offsets.
- **Card Surface (`#252F29`)**: Focal panels, architectural project cards, and reading planes.
- **Soft Charcoal (`#2D3630`)**: Popovers, contextual menus, and interactive surface states.
- **Muted Surface (`#343D36`)**: Active press states, selected chips, and badge backgrounds.

### Typography & Readability
- **Primary Text (`#F2EFE7`)**: Warm Ivory. Uncoated, tactile paper cast with absolute contrast against blackened surfaces.
- **Secondary Text (`#C0BBAF`)**: Weathered parchment. Used for architectural specs, narrative body text, and subtitles.
- **Muted Text (`#898C83`)**: Raw slate gray. Reserved for architectural metadata, footnotes, indexing numerals, and disabled states.

### Accents & Signatures
- **Champagne Gold (`#D8C19A`)**: Primary focal highlight. Used for active indicators, primary display markers, key CTA highlights, and editorial pull-quotes.
- **Antique Bronze (`#B89565`)**: Secondary accent. Used for secondary micro-interactions, decorative divider rules, and hover fills.
- **Muted Sage (`#929F87`)**: Natural counter-accent. Applied to landscaping notes, botanical tags, and subtle contextual statuses.
- **Warm Terracotta (`#B8755B`)**: Earth accent. Used sparingly for critical badges, material swatch highlights, or curated tags.

### Boundary Lines & Separators
- **Bronze Hairline (`rgba(216, 193, 154, 0.14)`)**: Deliberate framing borders around elevated cards and hero groupings.
- **Subtle Surface Hairline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Grid dividers, technical rules, and baseline demarcations.

## Typography

The typography system mirrors high-end architectural monographs: high contrast between romantic, seriffed literary titles and clinical, precise Swiss modernist specifications.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Sets an authoritative, poetic tone. Kept at regular to medium weights. Italic variations are encouraged strictly for curated project epigraphs, architectural locations, and thematic pull-quotes.
- **Narrative Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutralized, open-counter geometry engineered to retain warmth on deeply dark OLED and high-density screens. Offers effortless long-form reading for spatial descriptions and design histories.
- **Technical Metadata & Labels (`Inter`)**: Used in uppercase for indexing, dimension tags, material codes, blueprint legends, and interactive navigation elements. Generous letter-spacing gives an architectural plan aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical architectural grid that prioritizes structural tension, wide photographic vistas, and generous perimeter gutters.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column fluid grid governed by `margin: 3.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Editorial project showcases rely on wide multi-column spans (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired against an offset 4 columns for spec copy and floorplans).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-up presentation for image-text pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Content reflows vertically into structured stacked planes, with oversized typography scaling through dedicated mobile tokens to maintain vertical pacing.

Spacing rhythm strictly reinforces breathing room. Layouts must balance massive visual weight using the `space-xl` step between disparate narrative sections, while maintaining dense, technical grouping inside spec manifests via `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use drop shadows, blurred elevations, or stark white backlights. Depth is constructed through **Tonal Stacking**, **Hairline Demarcations**, and **Warm Ambient Illumination**.

1. **Surface Tiers**: Base content rests on `#111714`. Elevating content means stepping forward tonally through the palette: ground containers sit on `#18211C`, cards on `#252F29`, and active popovers on `#2D3630`.
2. **Hairline Bounding**: Surfaces are bound by razor-thin `1px` borders (`rgba(216, 193, 154, 0.14)` for highlighted editorial sections; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for systemic dividers). This mimics structural metalwork, fine joinery, and brass inlay reveals.
3. **Ambient Lighting**: Modals and prominent architectural cards utilize a faint, widely dispersed ambient radial bloom centered on interactive zones: a warm umber-tinted glow (`rgba(216, 193, 154, 0.035)` with a 48px blur radius), replicating the subtle wash of a concealed downlight against burnished timber.

## Shapes

The spatial language favors architectural precision, clean corners, and stone-cut structural profiles.

- **Foundational Geometry**: Default interactive elements and cards use `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / 4px). This softens harsh digital pixels without dissolving into organic, bubble-like, or overly casual curvature.
- **Specialized Radii**:
  - Modal sheets and primary editorial image frames apply `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
  - Chips, material badges, and status pills are permitted a pill profile when framing isolated specimen numbers or technical taxonomies.
- **Joinery Metaphor**: Dividers, line ends, and interactive focus states are strictly linear and orthogonal, echoing classical structural framing and bespoke millwork.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Champagne Gold (`#D8C19A`) fill with Midnight Black (`#111714`) typography in `label-md` uppercase. On hover, background shifts gracefully to Antique Bronze (`#B89565`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Corner radius: `0.25rem`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background transparent, surface bounded by `1px` Antique Bronze hairline (`rgba(216, 193, 154, 0.3)`), text in Warm Ivory (`#F2EFE7`). On hover, background fills with elevated tone `rgba(216, 193, 154, 0.08)`.
- **Editorial Text Button**: Pure typography with an explicit right-chevron or horizontal dash, rendered in Champagne Gold with a permanent micro-underline placed 4px below baseline.

### Cards & Specimen Displays
- **Architectural Project Card**: Constructed on Card Surface (`#252F29`) bordered by `1px solid rgba(216, 193, 154, 0.14)`. Aspect ratios for architectural imagery adhere rigidly to 4:3 or 16:9. Caption zones use `space-md` internal padding, featuring a `Playfair Display` header accompanied by tracking-spaced metadata in muted ivory.
- **Specimen Swatch Card**: Low-profile `#18211C` surface showcasing finish swatches (stone, walnut, bronze) flanked by hairline dividers separating visual samples from technical specification tables.

### Chips & Taxonomies
- **Material & Status Chips**: Container utilizes Soft Charcoal (`#2D3630`) with an inset `1px` boundary of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Text set in `label-sm` using Secondary Text (`#C0BBAF`). Selected state illuminates border to Antique Bronze with text in Primary Warm Ivory.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs & Textareas**: Resting state features Deep Forest (`#18211C`) fill with hairline outline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography defaults to `body-md` in Warm Ivory.
- **Focused State**: Outline transitions to crisp Champagne Gold (`#D8C19A`) with a subtle `1px` outer atmospheric illumination. Floating label transforms into `label-sm` uppercase positioned above the boundary.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Selectors**: Square `16px` frames with `2px` corners for checkboxes; circular rings for radios. Framed with hairline Antique Bronze (`#B89565`). Active selection uses Champagne Gold fill with an inset deep forest dot or ivory checkmark.

### Editorial Lists & Index Tables
- **Specification Index**: Horizontally striped ledger lists. Alternating or continuous transparent rows bounded by `1px` hairline rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Left side features technical indices (e.g., `01 / FACADE DETAIL`) in `label-sm` Muted Text, while right side details specs in `body-md` Warm Ivory. Hovering a row highlights the background to `#202A24`.

### Signature Component: Architectural Lightbox & Monograph Scroller
- A full-viewport horizontal project gallery that stacks high-contrast photography against Midnight Black surfaces, using delicate bronze numerical counters (`01`, `02`, `03` in `Playfair Display`) and vertical hairline dividers that trace viewport scroll depth.
