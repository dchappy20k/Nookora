Techniques & Protocols

Zero-Deposit-Risk DIY Guide

01

Interlocking Command Hook Gallery Matrix

Bangalore landlords frequently deduct ₹5,000 for drill holes. Instead, use interlocking 3M Command picture-hanging strips rated for up to 7.2kg. Wipe the standard PG oil-paint wall with rubbing alcohol beforehand to eliminate grease films.

02

The 2700K Warm Lighting Protocol

Never switch on the ceiling 6500K blue fluorescent tube. Unscrew it or leave the switch taped over. Place low-level amber illumination at three heights: a desk lamp at 3ft, a paper lantern floor lamp at 1.5ft, and concealed warm LED strip under the bed.

03

Concealing Wardrobe Laminate with Linen Tapestry

Aggressive cherry-red or faux-marble PG wardrobe laminates ruin room calmness. Hang washed oatmeal Khadi fabric over the wardrobe doors using magnetic clips or tension rods clamped inside the doorframe — zero tape residue, completely removable in 60 seconds.
